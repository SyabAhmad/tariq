## Draft website copy

**Hero line:** Entrepreneurship scholar, educator and university quality leader.

**Short bio:** Dr. Muhammad Tariq Yousafzai is an Associate Professor of Entrepreneurship and Director of the Quality Enhancement Cell at the University of Swat. His work connects entrepreneurship education, university governance and sustainable development. His research includes qualitative studies of value creation, informal recycling and climate-related livelihood risks in Pakistan.

**Longer bio:** Dr. Muhammad Tariq Yousafzai teaches and researches entrepreneurship at the University of Swat, where he also leads the Quality Enhancement Cell. He previously served as Director of the university’s Office of Research, Innovation and Commercialization. Across more than two decades of academic and consulting work, he has taught at FAST-NUCES, Sarhad University and the University of Peshawar. His research uses qualitative methods to examine entrepreneurship education, waste-picker livelihoods, circular economy practices and higher-education governance. He holds a PhD in entrepreneurship from Qurtuba University and an MS in Innovation and Business Creation from Jönköping International Business School.
